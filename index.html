01 · About

About Me

From MERN web apps to Python and Power BI analysis, I like building things people actually use, and finding what the data behind them says.

I'm an IT graduate (BSc Information Technology, Multimedia University of Kenya) who works across web development and data. I completed Full-Stack Development training with Power Learn Project, focused on the MERN stack, and I've built applications including a savings-group (chama) platform and a grievance redressal system with an AI chatbot.

On the data side, I clean, analyse and visualise data with Python, Pandas, SQL and Power BI. My final-year project combined NSE stock-market data with social-media sentiment analysis. My experience as a Growth Hacker & Software Developer at Wakandi Labs adds the growth side: email and SMS marketing, SEO and marketing analytics. I'm most interested in practical problems: tools people rely on every day, and data that helps them make decisions.

Chama Web App

A web platform for chamas (savings groups) in Kenya. It starts with a guided group-and-member onboarding flow and has sections for contributions, loans, savings goals and statements.
